## Universal requirements for every detailed SOP

1. Purpose and scope.
2. Trigger and prerequisites.
3. Required inputs and approved sources.
4. Exact ordered procedure.
5. Decision rules and exceptions.
6. Quality-control checklist.
7. Evidence or records to retain.
8. Definition of done and escalation path.
9. Owner and review cadence.
10. Version and change history.

## Non-negotiable controls

- No brief without query and intent research.
- No article without an approved SCN position and internal-link plan.
- No unsupported facts, fake testing, invented credentials, fake reviews, or fabricated data.
- No publishing without editorial and technical QA.
- No claiming indexing, ranking, citation, or deployment success without verification.
- Treat prompt tests as observations, not guaranteed ranking metrics.
- Apply local-business checks only where the site genuinely represents an eligible real local business.
